- **Ext, controls for a modern pad:** a new control style, as the decompilation's PC port plays a pad: the right stick looks and turns, the left stick walks and strafes, RT fires and LT aims, X reloads, Y and B change to the next and previous weapon, LB opens the weapon menu and a click of the left stick crouches. New agent files and new Combat Simulator players start on it; for a file you already have, pick Ext in Control Style, or 1.1 for the controls as the game shipped.
